Turn your match video into highlights and stats

MVA360 is desktop software for grassroots and junior soccer. Play the video, press T when something happens, and MVA360 builds the clips, highlight reels, match reports and season statistics for you.

From full-time whistle to highlights in an evening

You bring the video from a phone, action camera or auto-tracking camera. MVA360 does the rest on your own computer.

  1. 1

    Set up the game

    Choose your squad, the opponent and the match video, then tick who played and who started.

  2. 2

    Tag the moments

    Press T, pick the player and what happened. Or tag later: mark moments now and sort them after the game.

  3. 3

    Get the outputs

    Event clips, a match highlight reel, a highlight reel for each player and a match report, ready to share.

  4. 4

    Follow the season

    Season Centre adds up every game: results, minutes played, goals, tackles, saves and more.

Built for the sideline, not the analyst's office

Big buttons, keyboard shortcuts and plain language. If you can play a video, you can tag a game.

Tag as the game plays

Every player has a button and every event has a button. Each tag lands on the timeline, so you can jump straight back to any moment.

Coaches can also record substitutions, so MVA360 knows exactly who was on the pitch and for how long.

See the whole season

Results, recent form and season leaders update as you add games. Every player gets their own statistics, game by game.

Build an end-of-season highlight reel for any player from the moments you tagged all year.

Set your squad up once

Add players, shirt numbers and positions once. Every new game starts with the team sheet ready, and next season carries the same players across.

Your videos stay on your computer

Junior football footage is personal. MVA360 is desktop software, so nothing about your players leaves your computer unless you share it.

  • Nothing is uploadedMatch videos, player names, tags and statistics stay in the folder you choose.
  • Works at the groundTag without an internet connection. MVA360 only needs to check your plan once every 14 days.
  • Your originals are never changedMVA360 reads your video and saves clips and highlights as new files.
  • Your files are yours to keepClips, reels and reports are ordinary files. Keep them, share them or finish them in iMovie, even if you stop subscribing.

Questions

What do I need to run MVA360?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC, or a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 13 or later, and your match video as a normal video file (for example MP4 or MOV). A laptop is fine. Making highlight reels is faster on a newer computer.

Which cameras does it work with?

Any camera that saves a normal video file: phones, action cameras and auto-tracking cameras. MVA360 works from the downloaded video, so the camera brand doesn't matter.

How does the free trial work?

Your first game is free, with every feature. No card is needed. The trial is used when you select Start Match on your first game, and you can keep working on that game afterwards. To start another game, choose a plan.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. Everything you've made stays on your computer, and you can still open your existing games.

Do I need internet at the ground?

No. MVA360 works offline. It checks your plan in the background when you're online, and keeps working for 14 days without a connection.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes: Parent covers 1 computer, Coach 2 and Club 5. You can move your licence to a new computer from inside the app.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Email support@mva360.com.au and we'll switch you over, for example from Parent to Coach when you want Season Centre. Any unused part of your current plan is credited towards the new one.
