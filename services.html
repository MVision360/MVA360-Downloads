How it works

  1. Book. Click "Book a game" and fill in the email: your team, the date, your shirt colour, and the players and shirt numbers.
  2. Send the video. Share a link to the full match from Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or WeTransfer. Any normal video file works, from a phone, action camera or auto-tracking camera.
  3. We tag it. We confirm the price, then tag the game with MVA360.
  4. You get your files. We send a download link to the highlight reels and report, usually within 5 days.

Your video is deleted after 7 days

We only use your video to make your order. It's never shared, posted or used for anything else.

7 days after we send your files, we delete every copy of your video, along with the clips, reels, reports and player names we made from it. Download your files within that week. After that, we can't send them again.

Please only send footage you're allowed to share. If other children can be seen, check that your club or team is happy for games to be recorded and shared with families. See our privacy policy.

Questions

Do I need MVA360?

No. We use MVA360 to tag your game, and you just get the finished files. They're ordinary video files and a report that open on any phone or computer.

What if something's wrong?

Tell us within the 7 days, and we'll fix a missed moment or a wrong name at no charge. See cancellations and refunds.

Can I do it myself next time?

Yes. MVA360 is the same software we use. Try your first game free.
