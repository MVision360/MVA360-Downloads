How it works

  1. Book. Click "Book a game". An email opens with everything we need to know: club, team, age group, opponent, date, shirt colours, and each player's name, number and position. Fill it in and send it.
  2. We send Your Game Link. We reply with the price, how to pay, and Your Game Link: a private link just for your game, where you upload the video and collect your finished files.
  3. Pay and upload. Pay the invoice and upload the full match video using Your Game Link. Any normal video file works, from a phone, action camera or auto-tracking camera.
  4. We tag it. Tagging starts once payment is received.
  5. Collect your files. Your highlight reels and report appear on Your Game Link, usually within 5 days, and we email you when they're ready.

We can only tag what the camera recorded

We tag every moment we can see in your video. If something wasn't recorded, we can't tag it. That includes play that happened out of shot, while the camera was stopped, stuck or pointing the wrong way, or that was too far away, blocked or too dark to see.

We work out who's who from shirt numbers and the squad list you send. If a number can't be read, we use our best judgement and note anything we weren't sure about. Moments missing because of the recording aren't a mistake on our part, and aren't covered by our free fixes or refunds.

Your video is deleted after 7 days

We only use your video to make your order. It's never shared, posted or used for anything else. Only you and we can open Your Game Link.

7 days after your files are ready, we close Your Game Link and delete every copy of your video, along with the clips, reels, reports and player names we made from it. Download your files within that week. After that, we can't send them again.

Please only send footage you're allowed to share. If other children can be seen, check that your club or team is happy for games to be recorded and shared with families. See our privacy policy.

Questions

Do I need MVA360?

No. We use MVA360 to tag your game, and you just get the finished files. They're ordinary video files and a report that open on any phone or computer.

What if something's wrong?

Tell us within the 7 days. If we missed a moment that's clearly in the video, or got a name wrong, we'll fix it at no charge. See cancellations and refunds.

What makes a good video?

The whole match, filmed from the sideline and kept running, with the full pitch or the ball in view. Higher up is better. Auto-tracking cameras like XbotGo or Veo work well, and a phone on a tripod is fine too.

Can I do it myself next time?

Yes. MVA360 is the same software we use. Try your first game free.
