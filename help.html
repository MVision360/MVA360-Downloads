Using MVA360

1. Install MVA360

Windows

  1. Download the Windows installer from the download page and open it.
  2. Follow the steps. MVA360 installs just for you, so you don't need an administrator password.
  3. Open MVA360 from the Start menu.

Mac

  1. Download the Mac disk image and open it.
  2. Drag MVA360 into the Applications folder.
  3. Open MVA360 from Applications or Launchpad.

The first time MVA360 opens, it shows where your match data will be kept. Your first game is a free trial with every feature.

2. Set up your squad

A squad profile is your team for the season: player names, shirt numbers and preferred positions. You set it up once and reuse it for every game.

  1. Select Squad Profiles on the left, then New Squad Profile.
  2. Enter the team name and season, then add each player with + Add Player.
  3. Put the list in the order you like with ▲ Move Up and ▼ Move Down, or Sort by No.
  4. Select Save Squad Profile.

Parent plans include one squad each season, Coach plans two, and Club plans as many as you need.

3. Start a new game

Select Start New Game. There are three short steps.

  1. Match details: choose the squad, then enter the date, opponent and venue.
  2. Video and players: select Choose Video and pick the match video. Choose a tagging mode (see below), then tick the players available for this game. Changes here only affect this match, not your saved squad.
  3. Check and start: check the summary and select Start Match.

MVA360 never changes your original video. Clips, highlights and reports are saved separately.

4. Tag the match

Tagging marks the moments that matter: goals, shots, saves, tackles and so on. Choose one of two modes when you start the game:

  • Tag as you watch: press T when something happens, then choose the player and the event.
  • Tag later: press T to save the moment and keep watching. Afterwards, use Review Next Unclassified to choose the player and event for each saved moment. This is good for watching live or with the family.

Keyboard shortcuts

SpacePlay or pause
TTag an event (or save a moment in Tag later)
EscCancel a tag, or leave full screen
FFull-screen video
Ctrl+ZUndo the last tag

The ◀◀ 10s, ◀ 5s, 5s ▶ and 10s ▶▶ buttons jump back or forward. Everything saves as you go, so you can close MVA360 at any time and pick up later with Continue latest game.

To fix a tag, select it in the list and choose Review / Edit Selected (or double-click it). You can change the player, the event and how many seconds before and after the moment the clip includes.

5. Substitutions and minutes played

Coach and Club plans. Match Control records who is on the field, so MVA360 can work out each player's minutes.

  1. When the match kicks off in the video, open Match Control and choose Start Match. Tick the starting players.
  2. At each change, choose Substitution. You can record several players coming off and on at once with + Add Another Substitution.
  3. Record Half-time, Second-half Start and Full-time at the right moments in the video.

Made a mistake? Undo Last Match Control removes the last thing you recorded (a whole group of substitutions at once). To correct an earlier entry, select it in the history and choose Edit Selected Record.

If you forget Half-time or Second-half Start, MVA360 still counts the minutes and marks the match as estimated.

6. Clips, highlights and reports

When you've finished tagging, use the buttons under the event list:

Generate Event ClipsOne short video for every tagged event.
Generate Match Event HighlightsAll the events joined into one highlight video.
Generate Individual Player HighlightsA highlight video for one player. Great for sending to families.
Generate Match ReportA report with the score, statistics and team sheet that opens in your web browser.

Making videos takes a few minutes, depending on your computer. You can cancel at any time; clips that are already finished are kept. Use Open Game Folder to find everything you've made.

To leave an event out of clips and highlights, open it with Review / Edit Selected and untick Include in clip generation.

7. Season Centre

Coach and Club plans. Season Centre brings every game together: results, goals, current form, season leaders and each player's minutes.

  • View Player Statistics shows one player's season.
  • Generate Season Team Report makes a season summary for the team.
  • Build Season Player Reel makes an end-of-season highlight video for a player.

If the numbers look out of date after editing old games, select Rebuild Season Data.

8. Your plan and computers

Activate your plan

  1. After you subscribe, your licence key is shown on screen and emailed to you.
  2. In MVA360, click the Current access card at the bottom left.
  3. Paste the key and select Activate.

Using MVA360 offline

MVA360 checks your plan online about once a day. If you're offline, it keeps working for 14 days. Connect to the internet within that time and it checks again by itself.

Moving to a new computer

Parent covers 1 computer, Coach 2 and Club 5. To move MVA360, open the Current access card on the old computer and select Remove From This Computer, then activate on the new one. If the old computer is lost or broken, remove it from your subscription page instead (see below).

Changing plans

Email support@mva360.com.au and we'll switch you over. Your licence key stays the same, and any unused part of your current plan is credited towards the new one.

Cancelling or changing your card

Select Manage Subscription on the licence screen, or use the link in your licence key email. Enter your email and we'll send you a link to your subscription page, where you can cancel, update your card or remove a computer. If you cancel, MVA360 keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for. After that you can still open your existing games, but you can't start new ones. See cancellations and refunds.

9. Your data and backups

Your games, clips, highlights, reports and season data stay on your computer. Nothing is uploaded. Unless you choose another folder, they're kept in a folder called MVA360_Data in your user folder. Select Open Data Folder on the Dashboard to find it.

Backing up

Close MVA360, then copy the whole MVA360_Data folder to a USB drive or cloud storage. To restore it, copy it back, then choose that folder in Settings.

Don't keep the data folder inside Dropbox, OneDrive, iCloud or Google Drive. Syncing while MVA360 is open can damage the season data, and match videos take a lot of cloud space. MVA360 warns you if the folder is synced. Copy backups to the cloud while MVA360 is closed instead.

10. If something goes wrong

The video is black or won't play

This usually means the computer can't play that type of video. It often happens with iPhone video (HEVC) on Windows. MVA360 offers to Make a Compatible Copy. Your tags and times stay the same. It takes a while for a full match and needs free space about the size of the original video.

"The original video moved"

If you've moved or renamed the video file, MVA360 asks you to find it again. Choose the same video in its new place.

"Something went wrong"

Select Create Support Bundle… and email the file to support@mva360.com.au with a note of what you were doing. The bundle contains technical logs only: no videos, player names or match data. You can also make one any time from Settings → Create Support Bundle.

"This licence key is already in use on the maximum number of computers"

Remove MVA360 from a computer you no longer use (see moving to a new computer), then activate again.

Still stuck?

Email support@mva360.com.au. Tell us whether you use Windows or Mac and what you were trying to do. A screenshot helps.
